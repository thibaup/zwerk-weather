# Zwerk Weather logo

Created with the built-in image tool using the supplied icon as a style reference and the previous Zwerk logo as a colour reference. The final artwork is `zwerk-weather-logo.png`. The launcher uses five downscaled copies and a matching sun-and-cloud monochrome vector for themed icons.

## Design prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Create a redesigned Zwerk Weather Android launcher icon from these two references. Image 1 is a STYLE reference only: its simple rounded cloud and circular sun, soft smooth shading and uncluttered silhouette. Ignore the surrounding wallpaper and circular badge; do not copy the exact cloud shape. Image 2 is our CURRENT LOGO and COLOUR reference: cheerful blue background, soft white with pale blue cloud shading, warm yellow/amber sun.
Design: one original, softly sculpted cloud with a broad low profile, an asymmetric upper lobe and gently rounded base, with a plain warm golden sun disk peeking from behind its upper-right shoulder. Simplify to these two forms, without sun rays or rain strokes. Keep the cloud dominant and give the shapes gentle satin depth like the first reference, with restrained highlights and subtle shading. Calm but cheerful; no bright halo, bloom, harsh contrast, metallic gloss, dull grey palette or heavy shadow.
Background: a clean full-bleed blue-to-sky-blue gradient in our existing palette, slightly softer than the current icon. Do not bake in a circular or rounded-square badge: Android will mask it.
Composition: optically centered single emblem; every important sun and cloud edge inside the central 56 percent of the square, at least 22 percent clear background margin on every side. Crisp readable contours for small launcher sizes. Opaque square artwork only; no text, border, mockup, wallpaper, other symbols, or multiple versions.
```

## Launcher spacing refinement

```text
Edit only the size and placement of the weather emblem in this icon for Android adaptive-icon safety. Keep the exact same redesigned cloud outline, circular sun, colours, smooth shading, lighting and full-bleed blue gradient. Scale the complete cloud-and-sun group down by 20 percent, leaving the background full size, then center the group optically. Every solid cloud and sun edge must lie within the central 56 percent of the square, with at least 22 percent clear background on every side. Do not put a smaller rectangular picture inside the image; the existing blue background must seamlessly fill the entire square. No sun rays, rain strokes, glow, border, rounded corners, text, other objects or style changes. Return the single finished opaque square icon.
```
